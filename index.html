Research question

“What environmental burdens are produced by the activities and infrastructure that make campus life possible?”

What we observe

Noise

Everyday sound from movement, machinery, construction and campus activity.

Air

Exhaust, dust, smoke and emissions encountered in shared campus spaces.

Water

Litter, runoff and visible contamination around the campus environment.

A shared trade-off

Not all environmental burdens are equal.

A shuttle bus, canteen, construction site or social event may create environmental impacts while also serving an important campus function. The project therefore records both environmental burden and perceived value.
