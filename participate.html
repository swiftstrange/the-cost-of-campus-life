Participant manual

How to participate

Help make the everyday environmental trade-offs of campus life visible.

Five steps

01

Notice

Identify a noise, air or water pollution issue on NUS Kent Ridge Campus.

02

Locate

Mark where you experienced the issue.

03

Assess

Rate its intensity, disruption and the value of the activity or infrastructure producing it.

04

Document

Optionally add a photograph or short description.

05

Submit

Your observation is reviewed before becoming part of the collective dataset.

What can I report?

Noise

Traffic, shuttle buses, construction, machinery, events

Air

Exhaust, dust, smoke, cooking emissions

Water

Litter, runoff, visible contamination

Good submissions
  • relate to NUS Kent Ridge Campus
  • describe something actually observed
  • use the rating scales consistently
  • avoid photographing identifiable people where possible
